# Olleik videos — Creative brief V2 (replaces the "reference-style" promo look)

Status: Pending Review. Supersedes the visual/story direction of PROMO_COPY.md. Product captures, local demo stack, audio tooling and engine stay as they are.

## 1. What changed and why
The first pass was a slide-style SaaS promo (typed text, taps, cards). V2 must feel like a short film / motion-design ad: a story, people, rhythm, surprise. Not a presentation recorded as video.

## 2. Rules
- No slide look: no bullet lists, no centered title-over-screenshot, no "feature cards in a row".
- Every scene is driven by motion: match cuts, whip pans, shape morphs, speed ramps, object-to-UI transitions, kinetic type that moves with the action.
- Real product footage (already captured) appears INSIDE the story (on the chef's phone, on the warehouse screen), never as a full-screen slideshow.
- Music is optional. Lead with sound design (printer, stamp, box thud, phone buzz, knife on board, door bell). If music is used, keep it a quiet bed under effects. Ship a version with no music and one with.
- Never use real customer data. All names fictional (@olleik-demo.test data only).
- Keep the existing placeholders-out rule (no "[LEGAL NAME TBC]", no fake stats/testimonials, no "(000) 000-0000").

## 3. People (characters)
No stock footage, no real faces. Build 2D vector characters in Remotion/SVG (consistent, licence-free, fully animatable): flat shapes, warm paper palette, deep green + amber accents, simple rigs (head turn, arm reach, blink, apron/box props).
- Chef Marco: owner of a small restaurant. Tired, funny, ends relieved.
- Salma: warehouse lead at Olleik. Calm, precise, stamps tickets.
- Optional cameo: delivery driver (hands only, box handoff).
Option for the owner to decide later: AI-generated live-action people (Higgsfield) for the hero scene only. Costs credits, so do NOT run without explicit approval.

## 4. Promo story — "The night before" (hero cut 45–60 s)
1. 11:04 PM. Marco, empty kitchen, phone buzzing. Chat bubbles, voice-note waveforms, a crossed-out price and a missed call fly at the camera in kinetic type. Chaos rhythm, fast cuts. (hook, first 2 s must be the chaos, no logo)
2. One finger tap. A paper ticket prints; the flying bubbles snap into a clean grid (shape morph = chaos to catalog). Hard rhythm change.
3. The phone: real footage of catalog, own prices, three taps to place the order. Typing and taps drive the camera (push-ins, whip pans), line "Your catalog. Your prices." appears as moving type, not a title card.
4. Match cut: the printed ticket slides into Olleik. Salma tears it off, stamps it, boxes build themselves (animated shapes). Real admin footage on her screen, status chips flip.
5. 6:00 AM. Door bell, boxes land at Marco's door, sun through the window, he smiles. Red stamp SENT lands one beat before the end card: logo + URL.
Tone: warm, a bit funny, no corporate voice. On-screen words: few, big, moving.
Cuts to produce: 60 s hero, 30 s, 15 s vertical (chaos -> tap -> morning), 6 s bumper, 1:1 cut, and a loopable 10 s hero.
Alternative hooks to test (same story): "Still ordering supplies by text?" / "11:04 PM. One more text." / "Your supplier, in your pocket."

## 5. Usage videos (manuals) — same world, creative but clear
Principle: story-led walkthroughs with a character guide and tight motion, not a flat screen recording.
- Visitor manual: "Marco's first order" (~90 s). Marco as on-screen guide (small animated avatar in the corner reacting: points, nods, thumbs up). Camera follows the cursor/finger with smooth zooms; hand-drawn style callouts (circles, arrows, underlines) animate on instead of boxes; numbered steps become physical tickets that print. Chapters: apply, sign in, browse catalog, cart, checkout, order status, statements.
- Admin manual: split into short episodes (30–45 s each) instead of one 2:47 film: (1) Orders, (2) Customers and approvals, (3) Products and prices, (4) Margins and reports, (5) Invites and settings. Salma as the guide. Each episode opens with a one-line problem, shows the fix, ends with a "next" tease.
- Ship each with: burned-in captions (EN), separate SRT, no mandatory voiceover. Voiceover (TTS) only if approved.
- Staff manual stays skipped (no staff role).
- Reuse existing 3x footage; re-capture only if a scene needs a new angle.

## 6. Language
Default English on screen (matches the site). Arabic caption versions are a phase 2 option, only if the owner confirms.

3. After Go: final renders, loudness/sync QA, delivery to D:\olleik-videos\, REPORT.md updated, local demo DB removed. No merge without the owner's word.
